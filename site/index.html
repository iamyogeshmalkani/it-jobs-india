What we post

Every job passes the same checks before it reaches the channel.

In India

On-site, hybrid or remote, but based in India or open to candidates here.

Tech roles

Software, data, AI and ML, QA, DevOps, cloud, security and UI/UX. No sales or data-entry roles dressed up as "tech".

Entry level

Internships, trainee and fresher jobs. Senior and lead roles are filtered out.

Paid and safe

Unpaid internships and posts asking for fees, deposits or "WhatsApp only" are skipped.

How it works

Three taps from your phone to an application.

Join the channel

Open Telegram and join. New jobs appear in your feed through the day. Turn notifications on if you want to be first.

Tap “Apply now”

Each post shows the pay, location, skills and last date to apply, with an Apply button under it.

Apply on the official page

The button opens the company's own page or the listing on Unstop. You apply there, never through us.

Know a friend who is job hunting? Every post has a Share button that sends the job and the channel link.

Never pay anyone to get a job or internship.

A real employer does not charge for registration, training, a security deposit or "guaranteed placement". If someone asks for money or pushes you to chat only on WhatsApp, walk away. We filter these out, but no filter is perfect, so check any company before you share personal documents.

Questions, answered

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. Joining and reading the channel is free, and so is applying through the links we post. We will never ask you for money.

Where do the jobs come from?

From Unstop and from the public career pages and hiring tools of companies. We do not copy posts from other Telegram channels, and every post links back to the original listing.

How often do you post?

New jobs go out through the day, in small batches, so your feed is not flooded. Some days there are more, some days fewer, depending on what companies publish.

Do you guarantee a job or an interview?

No. We only help you find openings faster. Hiring decisions are made by the companies, and you apply on their pages.

What happens when a job closes?

When we find out a job has closed or its deadline has passed, we mark the post as closed so you do not waste time on it.

I found a job that looks suspicious. What do I do?

Do not pay or share documents. Message the channel admin on Telegram so we can look into it and remove it.
